# Dawnwardens

- **Type:** Knightly Order
- **Tags:** #faction, #knightly-order, #political

## Overview

The Dawnwardens are a prestigious order of knights and paladins dedicated to the ideals of justice, honor, and the protection of the innocent, tenets they draw from their devout faith in **[The Sovereign Flame](../Religions/The%20Sovereign%20Flame.md)**. They are the sworn guardians of the city of Eldoria and the surrounding lands, and are widely regarded as paragons of virtue and chivalry.

## Goals

- **Primary Goal:** To maintain peace and order in Eldoria and protect the city from all threats, both internal and external.
- **Secondary Goals:** To uphold the tenets of their faith and to serve as a beacon of hope for the common people.

## Structure & Hierarchy

The Dawnwardens are a strictly hierarchical organization, with a clear chain of command. At the top is the High Commander, who is responsible for the overall strategic direction of the order. Below the High Commander are the Knight-Captains, who lead the various companies of knights and paladins.

## Key NPCs

- **[High Commander Elara Vane](../Characters/High%20Commander%20Elara%20Vane.md):** The current leader of the Dawnwardens in Eldoria.
- **[Sir Gideon Thorn](../Characters/Sir%20Gideon%20Thorn.md):** A once-revered paladin of the order, now a fugitive.

## Allies & Enemies

- **Allies:** The common people of Eldoria, the local clergy.
- **Enemies:** The Scarlet Veil, criminal organizations, and any who threaten the peace of the city.

## Rumors & Lore

- "The Dawnwardens are not as pure as they seem. I've heard that some of their leaders are more interested in power than in justice."

## Role in Campaign

The Dawnwardens are the faction that initially hires the players to track down Sir Gideon Thorn. They represent the forces of law and order, but they are also a complex and nuanced organization, with their own internal politics and hidden agendas.
