# Scarlet Veil

- **Type:** Merchant Consortium / Criminal Syndicate
- **Tags:** #faction, #merchant, #criminal

## Overview

The Scarlet Veil is a powerful and enigmatic organization with a dual identity. To the public, they are a legitimate and highly successful merchant consortium, dealing in rare and exotic goods from across the world. In the shadows, however, they are a ruthless criminal syndicate, involved in everything from smuggling and extortion to assassination.

## Goals

- **Primary Goal:** To accumulate wealth and power, and to expand their influence in Eldoria and beyond.
- **Secondary Goals:** To eliminate their rivals, both legal and illegal, and to maintain a veil of secrecy around their illicit activities.

## Structure & Hierarchy

The Scarlet Veil is a labyrinthine organization, with a complex web of shell corporations, front businesses, and hidden cells. The true leadership of the syndicate is a closely guarded secret, with most members only knowing their direct superiors.

## Key NPCs

- **[Vesper Kane](../Characters/Vesper%20Kane.md):** A deadly assassin in the employ of the Scarlet Veil.

## Allies & Enemies

- **Allies:** Corrupt officials, smugglers, and other criminal elements.
- **Enemies:** The Dawnwardens, honest merchants, and anyone who threatens their bottom line.

## Rumors & Lore

- "The Scarlet Veil has eyes and ears everywhere. Be careful who you talk to in this city."

## Role in Campaign

The Scarlet Veil is the primary antagonist faction in the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. They are the ones who have been corrupted by the Heart of Shadows, and they are the ones who are seeking to claim its power for themselves.
