# Heart of Shadows

- **Type:** Wondrous Item
- **Rarity:** Legendary
- **Tags:** #item, #artifact, #cursed

## Description

The Heart of Shadows is a pulsating, crystalline orb of obsidian, roughly the size of a human heart. It is cool to the touch, and veins of a dark, viscous fluid can be seen swirling within its depths. The Heart exudes an aura of profound malevolence, and those who remain in its presence for too long are plagued by whispers of paranoia, ambition, and despair.

## History

The Heart of Shadows is an ancient and terrible artifact, the last remnant of a long-dead god of corruption and deceit. It was imprisoned ages ago by a circle of powerful druids, who sealed it away in a hidden grove in the Whisperwind Forest. The druids have long since vanished, but their powerful wards have kept the Heart dormant—until now.

## Role in Campaign

The Heart of Shadows is the central MacGuffin of the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. Its corrupting influence is the true source of the conflict, and its destruction is the only way to restore balance to the region. The Heart's power is seductive, and it will tempt the players with promises of power, even as it twists their minds and souls.

### Properties

- **Aura of Corruption:** The Heart radiates a powerful aura of corruption. Any creature that spends a prolonged period of time within 100 feet of the Heart must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or become paranoid and aggressive.
- **Whispers of Power:** The Heart whispers promises of power to those who hold it, offering them visions of conquest and glory. A creature that holds the Heart for more than a minute must make a DC 18 Charisma saving throw or become charmed by the artifact.
- **Destruction:** The Heart can only be destroyed by the combined power of a holy artifact and the sacrifice of a willing, pure-hearted soul.
