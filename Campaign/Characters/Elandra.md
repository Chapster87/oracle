# Elandra

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** High Elf Noble, Lawful Neutral
- **Tags:** #npc, #noble, #betrayer

## Appearance

Elandra possesses a serene and almost ethereal beauty, with long, midnight-black hair that contrasts sharply with her pale skin and striking violet eyes. She carries herself with an unshakable poise, her movements fluid and deliberate. She typically dresses in elegant, dark gowns that are fashionable in Lilrelond's court, favoring function and intimidation over simple aesthetics. Her expression is often unreadable, a carefully crafted mask of calm neutrality.

## Personality

Elandra is intelligent, ambitious, and politically savvy. She is a pragmatist who understands the brutal power dynamics of Lilrelond's court better than anyone. While she may have once felt genuine affection for Dal Cuinin, her actions are ultimately guided by a cold, calculating assessment of what is necessary for survival and advancement in her dangerous society. She is not needlessly cruel, but she is also not above making ruthless decisions to secure her own position.

## Role in Campaign

Elandra is the former love of Dal Cuinin who betrayed him to his brother, Torvin, leading to Dal's flight from Lilrelond. She remains in the elven capital, likely having secured a position of greater influence after her display of loyalty to the crown. She could reappear as an antagonist, a reluctant informant, or a complex neutral party if the campaign ever leads back to Lilrelond.
