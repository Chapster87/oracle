# Torvin

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** High Elf Fighter, Chaotic Evil
- **Tags:** #npc, #royalty, #deceased

## Appearance

Torvin was a fearsome warrior, built more like a battering ram than a graceful elf. He was broad-shouldered and heavily muscled, with a perpetually scowling face and a jaw that seemed permanently clenched. His silver hair was shorn short, and a brutal scar ran from his left temple to his chin. His eyes were a fiery, aggressive blue, quick to anger and contempt. He was always seen in battle-scarred black armor, preferring the practical gear of a soldier to the ceremonial dress of a prince.

## Personality

Torvin was the personification of Lilrelond's martial ferocity. He was arrogant, aggressive, and possessed an insatiable thirst for battle and conquest. He saw diplomacy as a tool for the weak and believed that the only true measure of worth was strength in combat. He was fiercely loyal to his father's vision of an elven-dominated world and was seen by many as the fist that would carry out the King's will. He was notoriously short-tempered and cruel, taking pleasure in dominating those weaker than himself.

## Role in Campaign

Torvin is a posthumous character, the deceased heir to the throne of Lilrelond. He was Dal Cuinin's older brother, and it was his confrontation with Dal that forced the latter to flee the kingdom. His death has created a power vacuum among the remaining princes and has likely intensified the internal struggles for King Yaelrion's favor. His legacy is one of brutality and ambition, and he serves as a grim reminder of the life Dal chose to escape.
