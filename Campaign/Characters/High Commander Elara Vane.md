# High Commander Elara Vane

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** Human Paladin, Lawful Good
- **Tags:** #npc, #dawnwardens, #commander

## Appearance

Elara Vane is a woman in her late forties, with sharp, intelligent eyes and a stern but fair expression. Her dark hair is streaked with grey and pulled back in a severe bun. She wears the immaculate, silver-and-gold armor of the Dawnwardens, but without the ostentatious flair of some of her subordinates. A long, thin scar runs the length of her left cheek, a souvenir from a long-ago battle.

## Personality

Elara is a pragmatist, driven by a fierce devotion to justice and order. She is a brilliant strategist and a capable warrior, but her greatest strength is her unwavering resolve. She can be perceived as cold and distant, but this is a deliberate mask to hide a deep-seated compassion for her people and a heavy sense of responsibility for her command.

## Role in Campaign

High Commander of the Dawnwardens in Eldoria, Elara Vane is the quest giver for the "Fallen Paladin" arc. She tasks the party with bringing Sir Gideon Thorn to justice, believing him to be a traitor. She is unaware of the full truth behind the Heart of Shadows, or at least, the extent of the cover-up within her own ranks.

## Secrets

- Elara suspects there is more to Gideon's story than meets the eye, but her duty to the Dawnwardens and the fragile peace with the Scarlet Veil forces her to act decisively. She may be a potential ally if the players can present her with irrefutable evidence.
