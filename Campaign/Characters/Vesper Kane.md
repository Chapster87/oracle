# Vesper Kane

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** Half-Elf Rogue, Lawful Evil
- **Tags:** #npc, #assassin, #scarlet-veil

## Appearance

Vesper Kane is a woman of indeterminate age, with a slender, athletic build and a dancer's grace. Her features are sharp and angular, and her eyes are the color of chipped ice. She moves with a silent, predatory confidence, and her smile is a thin, humorless line. She is always impeccably dressed in dark, tailored leathers, and she carries a pair of wickedly sharp, silver daggers.

## Personality

Vesper is a professional. She is a consummate predator, a patient and methodical killer who takes a quiet pride in her work. She is not cruel, but she is utterly ruthless, and she will not hesitate to eliminate anyone who stands between her and her target. She is a woman of few words, preferring to let her actions speak for her.

## Role in Campaign

Vesper Kane is a deadly antagonist in the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. She has been hired by the Scarlet Veil to assassinate Sir Gideon Thorn, and she will stop at nothing to complete her mission. She is a formidable foe, and her presence will add a constant sense of danger and urgency to the players' quest.

## Secrets

- Vesper's loyalty to the Scarlet Veil is purely transactional. She has no love for the faction, but they pay well, and they provide her with a steady stream of challenging assignments. She could be persuaded to abandon her contract, but only for a very, very high price.
