# Sir Gideon Thorn

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** Human Paladin, Lawful Good (Fallen)
- **Tags:** #npc, #paladin, #fugitive

## Appearance

Sir Gideon Thorn is a man in his prime, with a powerful build and the resolute bearing of a seasoned warrior. His once-bright blond hair is now matted and unkempt, and his face is etched with exhaustion and a deep, soul-weary sadness. His ornate Dawnwarden armor is battered and scorched, and he carries a heavy, two-handed sword that has seen better days. His eyes, however, still burn with a fierce, unwavering conviction.

## Personality

Gideon is a man of contradictions. He is a devout paladin who has broken his most sacred oaths. He is a hero who has committed a heinous crime. He is a man of faith who has been betrayed by his own order. Despite his fall from grace, he remains a deeply honorable and courageous individual, driven by a desperate need to protect the world from a threat that no one else will acknowledge.

## Role in Campaign

Sir Gideon Thorn is the central figure of the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. The players are initially tasked with hunting him down, but they will soon discover that he is not the villain he appears to be. He is a tragic hero, a man who has sacrificed everything—his honor, his reputation, and his freedom—to prevent a greater catastrophe.

## Secrets

- Gideon is not just a fugitive; he is a man on a sacred mission. He is racing against time to find and destroy the Heart of Shadows, an ancient artifact that is corrupting the Scarlet Veil and threatening to plunge the world into darkness.
- He is aware that some members of the Dawnwardens' leadership know about the Heart but are covering it up to avoid a war. This betrayal is a source of deep pain and anger for him.
