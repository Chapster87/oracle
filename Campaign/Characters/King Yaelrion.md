# King Yaelrion

- **Race, Class, & Alignment:** High Elf Fighter, Lawful Evil
- **Tags:** #npc, #royalty, #antagonist

## Appearance

King Yaelrion is a tall and imposing high elf, even in a race known for its grace. He has long, silver hair streaked with black, pulled back tightly from a face that seems etched from granite. His eyes are a cold, piercing grey, missing the characteristic warmth of his people. He is never seen without his ornate, black-and-silver plate armor, intricately engraved with the symbols of his lineage. His posture is rigid and militaristic, and he moves with the precise, deadly grace of a seasoned warrior.

## Personality

Yaelrion is a cold, calculating, and utterly ruthless ruler. He is driven by an unshakeable belief in elven supremacy and a deep-seated paranoia of the outside world. He sees weakness and corruption in all other races and even in elves who do not share his vision. He is a brilliant strategist and a master of intimidation, ruling Lilrelond with an iron fist. He values strength, loyalty, and ambition above all else, and has little patience for compassion or sentimentality, viewing them as weaknesses to be exploited.

## Role in Campaign

King Yaelrion is the primary antagonist from Dal Cuinin's backstory and a potential major threat to the wider world. He is the father of Dal Cuinin and the ruler of the xenophobic kingdom of Lilrelond. His goal is to amass enough power to launch a war of conquest and "purify" the world. He will likely serve as a powerful, background threat for much of the campaign, but could become a direct antagonist if the players draw his attention.
