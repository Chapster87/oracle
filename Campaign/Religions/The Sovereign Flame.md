# The Sovereign Flame

- **Type:** Faith
- **Tags:** #religion, #faith, #good, #lawful

## Overview

The Sovereign Flame is a faith founded on the principles of justice, truth, and righteous action. It is a monotheistic religion, centered on the worship of a single, benevolent deity who is believed to have gifted mortals with the divine spark of reason and morality.

## Domains & Teachings

The Sovereign Flame's primary domains are Light, Order, and Protection. Its core teachings emphasize the importance of compassion, integrity, and the courage to stand against the forces of darkness. Adherents are taught to be a light in the world, to champion the cause of the weak and the oppressed, and to uphold the rule of law.

## Worship & Rituals

Worship of the Sovereign Flame is a solemn and dignified affair, with services held in simple, unadorned temples. The most important rituals are the lighting of the sacred flame, which is said to symbolize the divine spark within each mortal, and the Oath of the Dawnwarden, a sacred vow of service and sacrifice.

## Clergy & Followers

The clergy of the Sovereign Flame are known as Flameseekers, and they are responsible for tending the sacred flames and providing spiritual guidance to the faithful. The most devout followers of the Sovereign Flame are the paladins of the Dawnwardens, who are considered the living embodiment of the faith's ideals.

## History & Myths

According to the faith's central myth, the world was once a formless void, until the Sovereign Flame brought light and order to the darkness. The myth tells of a great battle between the Sovereign Flame and a host of shadowy beings, and of the ultimate victory of the light.

## Role in Campaign

The Sovereign Flame is the guiding faith of the Dawnwardens, and its teachings are the source of their strength and their moral code. The faith is a powerful force for good in the world, but it is also a source of conflict, as its rigid adherence to the law can sometimes lead to intolerance and persecution.
