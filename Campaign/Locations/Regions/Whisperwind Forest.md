# Whisperwind Forest

- **Type:** Forest
- **Tags:** #location, #forest, #blighted

## Overview

Whisperwind Forest is an ancient, sprawling woodland, known for its towering, moss-draped trees and the constant, rustling whisper of the wind through its leaves. The forest is a place of primal beauty and quiet mystery, but a creeping blight has begun to poison the land, twisting the flora and fauna into grotesque parodies of their former selves.

## History

The forest has long been considered sacred by the reclusive communities of Firbolgs who call it home. They are the guardians of its ancient secrets, and their history is deeply intertwined with the woods. Legends speak of a dark power imprisoned beneath the forest floor, a power that the Firbolgs have guarded for generations.

## Points of Interest

- **The Firbolg Encampment:** A hidden community of Firbolgs, nestled deep within the forest. They are wary of outsiders, but they may be willing to help those who prove their worth.
- **The Blighted Grove:** The epicenter of the forest's corruption. Here, the trees are twisted and skeletal, the air is thick with a foul miasma, and the ground is littered with the bones of dead animals.
- **The Druid's Rest:** The ruins of a long-forgotten druidic circle, where the players can find clues about the Heart of Shadows and its connection to the blight.

## Key NPCs

- **[Sir Gideon Thorn](../../Characters/Sir%20Gideon%20Thorn.md):** The fallen paladin has taken refuge in the forest, seeking the aid of the Firbolgs and a way to destroy the Heart of Shadows.

## Rumors & Lore

- "The whispers on the wind have changed. They no longer sing of peace and tranquility, but of a growing darkness."
- "The Firbolgs are a myth, a children's story to keep us out of the woods. Or so I thought."
- "There's a power in that forest, older than the city itself. A power that should not be disturbed."

## Role in Campaign

Whisperwind Forest is a key location in the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. It is here that the players will learn the truth about Sir Gideon Thorn's mission and the true nature of the threat they face. The forest is a place of danger and discovery, where the players will have to navigate a treacherous landscape, contend with corrupted creatures, and earn the trust of the reclusive Firbolgs.
