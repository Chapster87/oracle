# Kingdom of Eldoria

- **Type:** Region
- **Tags:** #location, #region, #kingdom, #temperate

## Overview

The Kingdom of Eldoria is a prosperous and influential region in the heart of the continent. It is a land of rolling green hills, fertile river valleys, and well-tended farmlands, all centered around the bustling metropolis of [Eldoria](../Settlements/Eldoria.md). The kingdom serves as a major crossroads for trade and diplomacy, making it a melting pot of cultures and ideas. Its central location, however, also makes it a focal point for political conflict.

## History

The Kingdom of Eldoria rose from a humble trading post centuries ago, its growth fueled by its strategic location. The city of Eldoria itself grew into a powerful city-state, and its influence naturally expanded to encompass the surrounding lands. The kingdom has long been a battleground for competing ideologies, with various factions vying for control over its vast wealth and resources. The current era is defined by a tense peace, with the noble Dawnwardens and the enigmatic Scarlet Veil locked in a cold war for the soul of the kingdom.

## Inhabitants

The Kingdom of Eldoria is a cosmopolitan realm, with humans making up the majority of the population. However, due to its status as a trade hub, it is common to see individuals of all races in the city of Eldoria, including elves, dwarves, and halflings.

## Points of Interest

- **[Eldoria](../Settlements/Eldoria.md):** The capital city and the jewel of the kingdom. It is a center for trade, culture, and political intrigue.
- **The Golden Plains:** The vast, fertile plains that surround the city of Eldoria. They are the breadbasket of the kingdom, dotted with farms and small villages.
- **The River Iris:** A wide, navigable river that flows through the kingdom, connecting Eldoria to the sea and other major trade routes.

## Key NPCs

## Role in Campaign

The Kingdom of Eldoria is the primary setting for the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. It is a land of opportunity and danger, where political alliances are as sharp as any blade.
