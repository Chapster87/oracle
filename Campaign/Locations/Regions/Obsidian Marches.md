# Obsidian Marches

- **Type:** Region
- **Tags:** #location, #region, #mountains, #militaristic

## Overview

The Obsidian Marches are a formidable and unwelcoming region in the northeast, defined by jagged peaks of black volcanic rock and sharp, chilling winds that howl through the narrow passes. The land is harsh and unforgiving, with sparse vegetation and a sky that often seems bruised and overcast. The entire region is under the iron-fisted control of the elven kingdom of Lilrelond, and its forbidding landscape is a reflection of its rulers' isolationist and militaristic culture.

## History

The Obsidian Marches have been the domain of the elves of Lilrelond for millennia. The region was named for the vast quantities of black volcanic glass found there, which the elves learned to forge into weapons and armor of exceptional quality. The natural defensibility of the mountain ranges allowed them to develop their martial society in near-complete isolation. Over the centuries, they have fortified the few passes into the region, turning the entire land into a veritable fortress.

## Inhabitants

The Obsidian Marches are exclusively inhabited by the elves of [Lilrelond](../Settlements/Lilrelond.md). All other races are considered trespassers and are met with lethal force. The elven society here is rigid and hierarchical, with every individual's life revolving around service to the state and preparation for war.

## Points of Interest

- **[Lilrelond](../Settlements/Lilrelond.md):** The capital city and the heart of the elven kingdom. It is a fortress carved from a single, massive mountain of black stone.
- **The Blade Pits:** A massive quarry where obsidian is mined and initially shaped by laborers. It is a place of endless toil, guarded by stern elven warriors.
- **The Watcher's Spire:** A tall, slender tower on the southern border of the Marches, from which elven sentinels keep a constant, vigilant watch for any who would dare approach their lands.

## Key NPCs

- **[King Yaelrion](../../Characters/King%20Yaelrion.md):** The ruthless ruler of Lilrelond and the Obsidian Marches.

The Obsidian Marches is a looming threat to the north. It is the seat of power for King Yaelrion and a place of great danger for the player characters, especially [Dal Cuinin](<../../Players/Dal%20Cuinin%20(Kevin).md>).
