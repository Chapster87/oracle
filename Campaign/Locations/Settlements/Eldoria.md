# Eldoria

- **Type:** City
- **Region:** [Kingdom of Eldoria](../Regions/Kingdom%20of%20Eldoria.md)
- **Tags:** #location, #city, #trade-hub

## Overview

Eldoria is a bustling, cosmopolitan city, strategically located at the crossroads of several major trade routes. It is a melting pot of cultures, with towering spires of glass and steel mingling with ancient stone architecture. The city is a beacon of progress and prosperity, but beneath the gleaming facade lies a web of political intrigue, fierce rivalries, and long-buried secrets.

## History

Founded centuries ago as a humble trading post, Eldoria grew into a vital hub for commerce and diplomacy. Its strategic importance has made it a frequent battleground for competing factions, most notably the noble Dawnwardens and the enigmatic Scarlet Veil. The city has been rebuilt and fortified numerous times, with each layer of its history buried beneath the new. A fragile peace treaty, signed in the aftermath of a bloody turf war, has kept the city from open conflict for the last decade, but tensions remain high.

## Inhabitants

Eldoria is a true melting pot of cultures. While humans form the majority of the population, it is common to see individuals of all races on its streets, including a significant number of elves, dwarves, and halflings. The constant influx of travelers and traders makes the city a vibrant, if sometimes volatile, mix of languages, customs, and beliefs.

## Points of Interest

- **The Sunstone Citadel:** The heavily fortified headquarters of the Dawnwardens in Eldoria. It is a symbol of their power and a beacon of hope for the city's populace.
- **The Crimson Market:** A sprawling, semi-underground bazaar controlled by the Scarlet Veil. Here, one can find anything from rare spices and exotic beasts to illicit goods and forbidden knowledge.
- **The Diplomat's Rest:** The opulent hotel where the peace summit was held and Lord Malachar Voss was assassinated. It is now a crime scene, swarming with investigators from both factions.

## Role in Campaign

Eldoria serves as the inciting location for the "Fallen Paladin" story arc. The assassination of Lord Malachar Voss within its walls shatters the city's fragile peace and sets the players on the trail of Sir Gideon Thorn. The city is a powder keg of political tension, with both the Dawnwardens and the Scarlet Veil vying for control.
