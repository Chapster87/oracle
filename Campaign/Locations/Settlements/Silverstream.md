# Silverstream

- **Type:** Village
- **Region:** [Boreal Reach](../Regions/Boreal%20Reach.md)
- **Tags:** #location, #village, #isolated, #farming

## Overview

Silverstream is a small, secluded farming village nestled in a valley within [Boreal Reach](../Regions/Boreal%20Reach.md). It is a quiet, hardworking community, built around the banks of the titular Silverstream river, which flows down from the Frostfang Peaks. The village is characterized by its simple, sturdy wooden buildings, smoking chimneys, and the surrounding patchwork of fields that are diligently worked during the short summer months. The people here are insular but kind, valuing resilience, cooperation, and the bonds of community above all else.

## History

Silverstream was founded a few centuries ago by a handful of families seeking to escape the wars and politics of the southern lands. They chose the valley for its defensibility and the fertile soil near the river. The village has remained largely unchanged since its founding, its people living in harmony with the harsh seasons of the Reach. Its history is not one of grand battles, but of perseverance against brutal winters, of communities banding together to survive, and of quiet lives lived in a remote corner of the world.

## Inhabitants

The village is almost entirely populated by humans of hardy northern stock. The community is tight-knit and insular, with families having lived in the valley for generations. They are generally welcoming but cautious of outsiders.

## Points of Interest

- **The Old Mill:** A water-powered mill that is the heart of the village's grain production. It's a common gathering place for the villagers.
- **The Hearth and Home:** The single tavern and inn in Silverstream, run by a kindly old couple. It's a warm, welcoming place, full of the smell of woodsmoke and roasting meat.
- **Stillwater Farm:** The farm on the outskirts of the village where [Alakazam Shazam](<../../Players/Alakazam%20Shazam%20(Jeff).md>) was raised by a human couple.

## Key NPCs

- **Elara and Finn:** The aging human couple who found and raised Alakazam. They are simple, hardworking farmers with hearts of gold.

## Rumors & Lore

- "They say the Silverstream river is blessed. On the coldest winter nights, you can sometimes see the water glow with a faint, silver light."
- "Old Man Hemlock, up on the ridge, swears he saw a child walking in the snow during the great storm, the one that brought Alakazam. Says the child left no footprints."
- "The stones at the bottom of the Silverstream are unnaturally smooth and warm to the touch, even in the dead of winter."

## Role in Campaign

Silverstream is the hometown of [Alakazam Shazam](<../../Players/Alakazam%20Shazam%20(Jeff).md>) and the source of his values of order, community, and protection. It represents a peaceful, simple life that stands in stark contrast to the chaos and adventure he now finds himself in.
