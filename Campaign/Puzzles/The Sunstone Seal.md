# The Sunstone Seal

- **Status:** Active
- **Type:** Environmental, Combat
- **Tags:** #puzzle, #combat, #environmental

## Overview

The Heart of Shadows is sealed behind a massive stone door covered in faded, ancient runes. In the center of the door is a large, circular indentation. The chamber is circular, with several large, enchanted **sunstones** embedded in the walls, currently emitting only a dim, pulsing light.

## The Puzzle

The goal is to purify the seal on the door by focusing intense sunlight onto it.

1.  **Activation:** The players must figure out how to fully activate the sunstones. A successful **Intelligence (Arcana)** or **Wisdom (Religion)** check might reveal that the stones respond to radiant energy. A paladin's **Lay on Hands**, a cleric's **Sacred Flame**, or any spell that deals radiant damage channeled into the stones will cause them to flare to life, emitting bright beams of light.
2.  **Focusing:** The chamber contains several large, polished stone pillars that can be rotated. The players must rotate these pillars to act as mirrors, reflecting the beams of light from the sunstones and converging them all onto the indentation on the door.

## The Complication

As the light from the sunstones intensifies, it agitates the shadows in the room. **Shadow** creatures will coalesce from the corners and attack, creating a combat distraction that pressures the players while they try to align the pillars. This turns it into a dynamic encounter where some party members might need to hold off the shadows while others solve the puzzle.

## The Solution

Once all the light beams are focused on the door's indentation, the runes flare with pure, golden light, and the massive stone door grinds open, revealing the chamber containing the Heart of Shadows.

## Failure

There is no direct failure state for the puzzle, but the party will be overwhelmed by an endless stream of **Shadow** creatures if they take too long to solve it.
