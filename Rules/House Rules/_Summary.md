# House Rules

- [Flavor is Free](./Flavor%20is%20Free.md) - This rule encourages players to personalize how their abilities look, sound, and feel without changing their mechanical effects.
- [Exertion Casting](./Exertion%20Casting.md) - You can choose to incur levels of exhaustion to cast spell, despite having no available spell slots.
- [Combo Breaker (Coordinated Turns)](./Combo%20Breaker.md) - This rule allows player characters who roll the same initiative to act together, rewarding coordination, creativity, and teamwork with potential advantage.
- ["I Know a Guy"](./I%20Know%20A%20Guy.md) - Allows players to retroactively establish connections in the world through roleplay. It encourages improvisation, character history, and emergent storytelling—while keeping control firmly in the DM’s hands.
- [Potion Maxing](./Potion%20Maxing.md) - This rule gives players a meaningful choice when using potions: drink quickly for uncertainty, or take time for guaranteed results.
