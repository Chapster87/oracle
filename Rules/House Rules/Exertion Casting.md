# Exertion Casting

**What This Rule Does**  
When you have no spell slots left, you can still cast—by taking levels of Exhaustion. This is intended for desperation magic, not routine play.

## Exertion Casting

When you cast a spell using a spell slot, you normally expend a slot of the spell’s level.  
With this rule, a creature can attempt to cast a spell without an available spell slot by suffering Exhaustion.

## Eligibility

You can use Exertion Casting when _all_ of the following are true:

- You are able to cast spells using spell slots.
- You are attempting to cast a spell of 1st level or higher.
- You have no remaining spell slots of that spell’s level.

If you meet those conditions, you may cast the spell and gain Exhaustion levels based on the spell’s level.

### Exhaustion Cost by Spell Level

| Spell Level | Exhaustion Gained    |
| ----------- | -------------------- |
| 1st–3rd     | +1 Exhaustion level  |
| 4th–6th     | +2 Exhaustion levels |
| 7th–9th     | +3 Exhaustion levels |

### Lethal Threshold

If Exertion Casting would increase your Exhaustion to 6, you cast the spell and then die immediately after the spell is resolved.

## Additional Rules

- **Components**: You must still provide all components required to cast the spell.
- **Concentration**: If the spell requires concentration, you can concentrate on it normally. If you gain Exhaustion during the casting, that does not automatically break concentration unless your Exhaustion rules cause you to fail concentration checks later.
- **No Discounting the Cost**: The Exhaustion gained from Exertion Casting cannot be reduced, avoided, or ignored unless a feature explicitly says it removes or prevents Exhaustion.
- **Stacking Exhaustion**: If you already have Exhaustion, add the levels gained from Exertion Casting to your current level.

## Choose Your Exhaustion System

This rule works with either exhaustion model below. Pick one for your campaign and use it consistently.

### Exhaustion (2014 Rules Summary)

Exhaustion has 6 levels. Each time you gain Exhaustion, your level increases by the amount gained. You suffer your current level’s effects and all lower levels’ effects.

| Level | Effect                                         |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Disadvantage on ability checks                 |
| 2     | Speed halved                                   |
| 3     | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4     | Hit point maximum halved                       |
| 5     | Speed reduced to 0                             |
| 6     | Death                                          |

**Recovering Exhaustion**  
Finishing a long rest reduces Exhaustion by 1 level, provided you have also had food and drink.

### Exhaustion (2024 Rules Summary)

Exhaustion is cumulative. Each time you gain it, you gain 1 Exhaustion level (or more, if an effect says so). You die at 6.

**D20 Tests Affected**  
When you make a D20 Test, the roll is reduced by 2 × your Exhaustion level.

**Speed Reduced**  
Your Speed is reduced by 5 feet × your Exhaustion level.

**Recovering Exhaustion**  
Finishing a long rest removes 1 Exhaustion level. When your Exhaustion level reaches 0, the condition ends.
