# Combo Breaker (Coordinated Turns)

**What This Rule Does**  
This rule allows player characters who roll the same initiative to act together, rewarding coordination, creativity, and teamwork with potential advantage.

> **Advantage is granted at the DM’s discretion and should reward clever coordination, not routine or repetitive actions.**

## Combo Initiative (Coordinated Turns)

When initiative is rolled at the start of combat, ties can feel awkward or inconsequential.  
With this optional rule, when two or more player characters roll the **same initiative number**, they may choose to take their turns together, acting as a coordinated unit.

## Declaring a Combo Turn

When player characters share the same initiative count, they may declare a **Combo Turn** at the start of that initiative.  
To declare a Combo Turn:

- All participating players must agree.
- The DM must approve the coordination as reasonable.
- The players briefly describe how their actions work together.

Once declared, the involved characters resolve their turns simultaneously.

## Resolving Combo Actions

During a Combo Turn:

- Each character still uses their **own action economy** (action, bonus action, movement, reaction).
- Actions may be interwoven narratively and tactically.
- If order matters mechanically, the DM determines the sequence or allows players to choose.

## Advantage for Effective Combos

If the DM determines that the coordinated actions:

- Clearly support one another
- Are thematically or tactically cohesive
- Create a meaningful setup or payoff

Then **one or more rolls** made as part of the combo may be made with **advantage**.

---

### Examples of Effective Combos Include:

- One character restraining or repositioning an enemy while another strikes
- A spell setting up terrain or conditions for a follow-up attack
- Coordinated movement that traps or flanks a target
- A distraction paired with a decisive blow

---

### Restrictions

- **Combo Initiative** only applies when initiative results are **exactly the same**.
- Advantage granted through this rule applies only to rolls **directly tied to the combo**.
- This rule does **not grant** extra actions, additional attacks, or automatic success.
- NPCs and enemies may use this rule at the DM’s discretion.

---

### DM Guidance

Combo Initiative should feel special. If players are coordinating creatively, reward them. If they attempt to fish for advantage every round, withhold it. This rule works best when advantage feels **earned, not expected**.
